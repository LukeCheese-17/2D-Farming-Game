# Ideas

A big pile of ideas for the creepy cozy farm, from tiny polish to game-changers. Nothing here
is a plan; pick what you like. Each idea is tagged with a rough size:

- **[S]** small: an evening or less
- **[M]** medium: a few sessions
- **[L]** large: a new system or a big chunk of content
- ⭐ = my favourites

They all try to respect the rule that **nothing ever attacks or appears on the surface** (ATM-1):
up top the horror stays in what you *notice*, not what you *see*.

---

## My top 12 picks

1. ⭐ **The scarecrow that moves** [S]. It stands somewhere slightly different each morning, always facing the house. (Atmosphere)
2. ⭐ **A journal** [M]. Your character writes one line per day by themselves, and the lines slowly get stranger. (Story)
3. ⭐ **Weather: fog days, rain and storms** [M]. Rain waters your crops for you; fog days have more cues. (Farming, Atmosphere)
4. ⭐ **Sprinklers** [M]. The quality-of-life upgrade every farming game needs. (Farming)
5. ⭐ **Animal care and friendship** [M]. Pet them, and happy animals give better products. (Animals)
6. ⭐ **The boarded house's secret** [L]. The fourth house finally opens at the end of the main quests. (Story)
7. ⭐ **Crop quality** [M]. Normal, good and *strange* crops. The strange ones sell for more and look a little wrong. (Farming)
8. ⭐ **A cooking pot in the house** [M]. Combine ingredients into meals that refill more or give buffs. (Food)
9. ⭐ **Fish journal and animal journal** [S]. Gotta-catch-them-all collection pages. (Collection)
10. ⭐ **Mailbox letters with no sender** [M]. A second, quieter story channel next to the phone. (Story)
11. ⭐ **Quick travel from the signposts** [S]. Fewer long walks at night. (Quality of life)
12. ⭐ **An ending** [L]. Several endings, depending on what you did and didn't do. (Story)

---

## 1. Game-changers

- ⭐ **Several endings** [L]. After the last main quest, the caller asks you to "come down to the bottom of the mine one last time". What happens depends on choices you made: whether you ever named him, how many animals you lost, whether you opened the boarded house, whether you went to floor 5 at night.
- **Seasons** [L]. Spring, summer, autumn and winter, 14 days each. Crops are tied to seasons, winter has snow and only a few cold crops, and the map changes colour. (Currently out of scope, but it's the classic next big step.)
- ⭐ **Weather system** [M]. Clear, overcast, rain, fog and storms. Rain waters every tilled tile. Storms knock over a few fences (gates swing open!), which ties into the animal escape mechanic. Fog days push the darkness and cue rate up.
- **A second farm plot** [L]. A greenhouse or a fenced field across the river, unlocked by a main quest. The greenhouse ignores seasons and weather.
- **Night shift** [L]. Some things only happen at night: night-only crops (moonflowers), night fishing spots, mushrooms that glow. You'd stay up and risk the 02:00 pass-out for better rewards.
- **Sanity / unease meter** [L]. Hidden or shown as a subtle screen effect. It goes up in the dark, deep in the mine, or when you ignore the phone, and down when you sleep, eat a good meal or pet animals. High unease = more cues, stranger dialogue and slightly wrong UI (item names misspelt, the clock jumping). It never kills you; it just makes the world weirder.
- **Choices with the caller** [L]. Sometimes he asks for something odd ("Bury the next egg you find by the dead tree"). Doing it or refusing changes later quests and the ending.
- **Village repopulation (without people)** [L]. As buildings come back, *signs* of life return, but you never see anyone: lights on in windows, fresh footprints, washing on lines, a kettle steaming, a radio playing in an empty room.
- **New Game+** [M]. Keep your tools and journal, but the world is "wrong" from day 1: the house is a mirror image, the caller already knows your name, the follower is in more places.
- **Multiple save "lives"** [M]. Each slot remembers how the other slots ended. A note in slot 2's mailbox: "Did it go better last time?"

## 2. Story and mystery

- ⭐ **Journal** [M]. Opened from the pause menu. One automatic entry per day based on what happened ("Caught a weeping gar. It cried."), plus fragments the player finds. Late entries are in different handwriting.
- ⭐ **The boarded house** [L]. Nailed shut "from the inside". After the last main quest, the planks are gone one morning. Inside: a small room with the same layout as your farmhouse, a bed made up for you and a phone that's ringing.
- ⭐ **Mailbox at the farm gate** [M]. Letters from no one: old village newsletters, a seed catalogue with one item circled, a letter addressed to the *previous* owner, a postcard with a photo of your house taken from the tree line last night.
- **Collectible notes** [M]. Scraps hidden around the map and in the mine (under rocks, in fish bellies, in the bottom of a feed sack). Put together, they tell what happened to the village.
- **Grave plots** [S]. A tiny overgrown graveyard in the woods. The names match the village shop signs. One headstone is blank, and later it isn't.
- **The tally on the feed store door** [S]. It stops at forty, but each time you lose an animal there's one more mark.
- **The previous owner** [M]. Find their things: a hat on a fence post, their old rod at the pond (better than yours, but it pulls left), a diary in the mine on floor 3.
- **Photographs** [M]. Find a camera. Taking pictures of places sometimes shows something in the developed photo (the next morning) that wasn't there. Still no monster on screen: only in the photos.
- **Radio** [M]. A radio in the house plays weather forecasts and old music. Late at night it's a voice reading out what you did today.
- **Missing posters** [S]. Faded posters in the village that slowly get updated, one of them for an animal you lost.
- **The caller's backstory, never confirmed** [S]. Contradictions you can notice: he mentions things he couldn't know, the phone line is cut but he still calls, he laughs at the wrong moment.
- **Day counters** [S]. On day 100, 200 and 365, something special happens (a cake on the doorstep with no candles, every animal lying down at once).

## 3. Atmosphere and horror (surface stays monster-free)

- ⭐ **Scarecrow** [S]. Buy or build one for the field. It keeps crows away, but it's in a slightly different place every morning and always faces the house.
- **Crows** [M]. Without a scarecrow, crows sometimes eat a seedling. They gather on the fence and all leave at once for no reason.
- **Wrong footprints** [S]. A trail of bare footprints in the dew in the morning, from the tree line to your window and back.
- **Knocking** [S]. Three knocks on the door while you're inside. Nobody's there. Rarely, it knocks from the *inside* of the wardrobe.
- **Your own name** [S]. Once the caller is named, the wind sometimes whispers the *player's* name.
- **Things rearranged** [S]. More "moved overnight" cues: hotbar slots swapped, a tool on the bed, a fish in the shipping box you didn't put there (it doesn't sell).
- **The extra crop** [S]. A crop you didn't plant ripens in the middle of your field. Its harvest is an item called "?" that sells for 1g and comes back in your bag if you throw it away.
- **Mirror** [S]. A mirror in the house. The reflection lags half a second behind.
- **Lights in the woods** [S]. A lantern light in the trees at night that goes out when you walk toward it.
- **Silence events** [S]. All ambient sound stops for ten seconds, then resumes. No explanation.
- **Animals refuse** [S]. Some mornings an animal won't come out of a corner of the pen and just faces the fence.
- **The well** [S]. Sometimes the bucket comes up with something: a key, a tooth, a wet photograph of you.
- **Fog walls** [M]. On fog days the fog hides most of the map; sound carries further, and the follower's steps are closer.
- **Window cue** [S]. Something is standing just outside the window when you wake up. You only see its shadow on the floor, and it's gone the moment you move.
- **Clock skips** [S]. Very rarely the clock jumps forward ten minutes, and the journal has an entry for those missing minutes.
- **Dead tree** [S]. The big dead tree has more ribbons tied to it every week.
- **Sleep paralysis morning** [S]. Rarely, the morning starts with a few seconds where you can't move and the screen is dark, with breathing. Then it's a normal day.
- **Unease scales with day count** [S]. Cues get a bit more frequent the longer the save goes on.

## 4. Farming

- ⭐ **Sprinklers** [M]. Basic (4 tiles), quality (8) and iridium-style (24) from the blacksmith. Big QoL.
- ⭐ **Crop quality** [M]. Normal, good, and "strange" (a rare purple tint, sells for 2x). Fertiliser raises the chances.
- **Fertiliser** [S]. Made from animal products or bone meal from the Bone Gallery. Speeds growth or raises quality.
- **Regrowing crops** [S]. Tomatoes, berries and beans that keep producing after the first harvest.
- **Giant crops** [M]. 3×3 pumpkins that sometimes form when nine are planted together. Need the axe to harvest.
- **Trellis crops** [S]. Grapes, hops and beans that block movement, so you plan paths through your field.
- **Fruit trees** [M]. Plant saplings that take weeks to grow but give fruit forever.
- **Wilting** [S]. Crops that miss two days of water wilt and lose a growth step (only with no rain).
- **Weeds come back** [S]. Weeds slowly spread onto untilled farm tiles overnight, so clearing them matters.
- **Seed maker** [M]. Turns a crop back into a few seeds.
- **Night crops** [S]. Moonflowers and pale melons that only grow if harvested at night.
- **Crop that grows toward you** [S]. One cursed crop leans in the direction of wherever the player is standing.
- **Scythe tool** [S]. Harvest and clear weeds in an arc instead of one tile.
- **Bigger hoe/can sweeps** [S]. Upgraded hoe and can affect 3 tiles (charge up by holding the button).
- **Compost bin** [S]. Put in bad items (rotten food, weeds), get fertiliser a few days later.

## 5. Animals

- ⭐ **Care and friendship** [M]. Pet each animal daily (hearts over it). Higher friendship = better products, and they follow you along the fence.
- **Feed and hay** [M]. Cut grass with a scythe for hay, store it in a silo; animals that aren't fed don't produce.
- **Coop and barn buildings** [L]. Animals sleep inside at night, which also protects them from "disappearing", even if the gate is open.
- **Baby animals** [M]. Buy chicks and calves cheaper; they grow up over a few days.
- **Breeding / incubator** [M]. Hatch eggs in an incubator. Very rarely, the chick that hatches is black and never makes a sound.
- **Naming animals** [S]. Name them when you let them out. The morning report then says "*Bessie* is gone", which hurts more.
- **Animal bells** [S]. Put a bell on an animal; you can hear where it is. Some nights you hear the bell walking away from the pen.
- **Pigs and truffles** [M]. A pig that digs up truffles in the woods (and occasionally other things).
- **Bees** [M]. Beehives near flowers for honey; flower type changes the honey.
- **Horse** [L]. Ride it to move faster. It refuses to go near the mine.
- **Dog or cat** [M]. A pet that sleeps in the house and growls at the tree line at night. It never finds anything.
- **Returned animals** [S]. Very rarely, a lost animal comes back a week later. It's fine. It doesn't make any sound.
- **Products from happy animals** [S]. Large eggs, rich milk and fine wool, each worth more.
- **Mayonnaise / cheese / cloth machines** [M]. Artisan machines that turn products into higher-value goods.

## 6. Fishing

- ⭐ **Fish journal** [S]. Shows every fish, where and when it bites, and the biggest you caught.
- **Fish sizes** [S]. Each catch has a size; bigger ones sell for more and go in the journal.
- **Bait and tackle** [M]. Bait makes bites come faster; tackle widens the lit zone.
- **Crab pots** [M]. Leave them in water, collect each morning (crabs, junk, and once, a wedding ring).
- **Junk catches** [S]. Boots, tins, a soaked letter, a doll's head.
- **Legendary fish** [M]. One per water body, very hard, with a little story in its description.
- **Ice fishing** [M]. In winter (if seasons happen), cut a hole in the frozen pond.
- **Mine fishing** [S]. An underground lake on Frozen Dark with blind white fish.
- **Something big in the lake** [S]. A huge shadow passing under the bobber that never bites.

## 7. The mine

- **Mine elevator save points every 5 floors** [S]. If more floors get added.
- **More floors / an endless "deeper"** [L]. Below floor 5, floors are randomly mixed, harder and stranger, endless for score-chasing.
- **Treasure chests** [S]. Rare chests with rings, scrolls or old village items.
- **Rings / trinkets** [M]. Equip one or two for small buffs: light radius, less hunger, faster swings.
- **Lanterns and light radius** [M]. Deeper floors are darker; buy better lanterns at the blacksmith.
- **Bombs** [S]. Clear rocks in an area.
- **Gems and geodes** [M]. Crack geodes at the blacksmith for surprise minerals.
- **Mine events** [S]. A tunnel that wasn't there before, a lit candle on a floor no one has been to, your own footprints ahead of you.
- **Boss at the bottom** [L]. "Something waits at the bottom of floor 5". Make that a proper encounter (it never comes to the surface).
- **Mining achievements** [S]. "Reach floor 5 without a sword", "Leave the mine with 1 HP".
- **Mine carts** [M]. Shortcut rides between floors.
- **Enemy journal** [S]. Sketches and short notes on each enemy as you fight them.

## 8. Village

- **Rebuildable extras** [M]. A bakery (bread, pies), a tailor (wool → clothes for the player looks), a clinic (healing items), a museum (donate fish, minerals and notes).
- ⭐ **Museum / collection hall** [M]. Donate one of every fish, mineral and note; rewards for milestones.
- **Bulletin board history** [S]. Old quests stay pinned underneath the new ones, yellowing.
- **Village lights at night** [S]. Rebuilt buildings light their windows at night, but the doors are locked after dark.
- **Festival days** [M]. A harvest festival where tables are set out with food for people who never come. You can enter crops for a prize that's waiting on the table the next morning.
- **Shop opening hours** [S]. Shops are only open certain hours, which makes the day planning more interesting.
- **Shop stock rotation** [S]. A few random items each day.
- **Church** [L]. A bell tower you can rebuild. When it's finished, it rings at midnight. Sometimes it rings 13 times.

## 9. Phone and the caller

- **More questions** [S]. "Why me?", "Where are you calling from?", "Is anyone else out here?", each with several vague answers that rotate.
- **He remembers** [S]. He comments on things you did today ("You were by the river a long time").
- **Calls from other numbers** [S]. A wrong number asking for the previous owner. A call from your old job.
- **Call log** [S]. A list of past calls in the phone menu, with one call you don't remember answering.
- **Supply order upgrades** [S]. As the story goes on, he offers more items to order (fertiliser, fences).
- **Voicemail** [S]. Missed calls leave messages; some are just breathing, some are you.
- **Phone with a cord** [S]. You can walk only a step or two away while on the phone (purely visual).

## 10. The house

- **House upgrades** [L]. Kitchen, bigger room, cellar (aging wine/cheese). Built by "someone" overnight like the village.
- **Furniture and decorating** [M]. Buy and place furniture. Some furniture doesn't stay where you put it.
- **Storage chests** [S]. Huge QoL; place chests in the house and on the farm.
- **Fridge** [S]. Food stored in the fridge counts for cooking without being in your bag.
- **Wardrobe to change looks** [S]. Swap between player looks in-game instead of only from the menu.
- **Bathroom / bath** [S]. Taking a bath restores a bit of unease (if the unease meter happens).
- **Calendar on the wall** [S]. Shows the day, and days you crossed out yourself (some you didn't).

## 11. Economy and progression

- **Price changes** [M]. Sell prices drift day to day; selling too much of one thing drops its price.
- **Shipping records** [S]. A page that shows totals per item and money earned over time.
- **Bank or debt** [M]. You started broke; maybe you owe the caller for the land, and payments come due every week.
- **Skill levels** [M]. Farming, fishing, mining and foraging levels with small perks per level.
- **Achievements** [S]. Steam-style list in the menu.
- **Shop for player looks** [S]. Hats, clothes and colours bought with money.
- **Gold star goals** [S]. Optional weekly goals from the board ("Ship 50 eggs this week").

## 12. Tools and crafting

- **Crafting menu** [M]. Make fences, chests, sprinklers, scarecrows, torches and paths from materials anywhere, not just at shops.
- **Paths you can place** [S]. Lay stone or wood paths on the farm (faster walking, MOV-7).
- **Torches and lamps** [S]. Place light sources on the farm; the dark is less scary near them.
- **Tool upgrades for the hoe and can** [S]. Copper/iron/gold hoe and watering can (bigger areas, more water).
- **Watering can capacity** [S]. Refill at water; adds a small trip.
- **Fishing rod upgrades** [S]. Wider zone, faster bites, can use bait.
- **Repair the house** [M]. Use wood and stone to patch holes in the walls and roof, which visually changes the house.

## 13. Weather and time

- ⭐ **Rain** [M]. Waters crops, fills the well, quieter fishing but more fish.
- **Fog** [S]. Already have fog layers; make some days *thick* fog days.
- **Storms** [M]. Lightning hits a tree (wood bonus), knocks fences down, and the power goes out in the house (the phone still rings).
- **Weather forecast** [S]. On the radio or the phone ("Tomorrow is going to be... quiet.").
- **Days of the week** [S]. Show Mon–Sun so shops and events can follow a weekly schedule.
- **Moon phases** [S]. Full moon nights = more cues and better night fishing.

## 14. Quality of life

- ⭐ **Quick travel** [S]. The signposts get a "Go to..." menu (home, village, mine) that costs an hour of game time. The trip happens off screen, and sometimes you arrive with mud on your boots from somewhere you didn't go.
- **Sleep from anywhere after midnight** [S]. An option to "lie down here" outside, at a cost (wake up hungrier, something moved).
- **Auto-pickup radius** [S]. Items within a tile or two get collected automatically.
- **Hold to repeat actions** [S]. Hold the use key to keep tilling/watering in the facing direction.
- **Stack split** [S]. Right-click a stack in the backpack to pick up half.
- **Sort backpack button** [S]. Sort by type.
- **Trash slot** [S]. Bin items you don't want.
- **Ship from the bag** [S]. Drag any item from the backpack onto the shipping box, not just from the hotbar.
- **Item tooltips with more info** [S]. Show food/water values, where a fish lives, which quest wants it.
- **Mini-map / map screen** [M]. A hand-drawn map in the pause menu that fills in as you explore. Your house is labelled in someone else's handwriting.
- **Clock warnings** [S]. A small warning at 00:00 and 01:30 before the 02:00 pass-out.
- **Need warnings** [S]. A gentle nudge when food or water is about to run out.
- **Confirm before selling special items** [S]. Don't let the player ship a quest item by accident.
- **Show which crops need water** [S]. Option to tint dry crops.
- **Gate prompt** [S]. Show "Use: Open gate" / "Close gate" in the prompt line when facing a gate.
- **Highlight pens** [S]. While holding a crate, faintly highlight every closed pen on screen.
- **Counts in shops** [S]. Show how many of an item you already own in the shop menu, and buy 5 / 10 at once.
- **Remember the last hotbar slot per tool** [S]. After eating or fishing, jump back to what you were holding.
- **Key rebinding** [M]. In Options.
- **Controller support** [M]. Gamepad movement and menus.
- **Autosave indicator** [S]. A small icon when the game saves.

## 15. UI and feel

- **Juice** [S]. Tiny particles when tilling, splashes when watering, wood chips when chopping, a little bounce on harvested crops.
- **Screen shake on big hits** [S]. Only in the mine.
- **Day-start card** [S]. "Day 12 — Wednesday — fog" shown as you wake up.
- **End-of-day summary screen** [M]. Pretty version of the morning report with icons and totals.
- **Animated title screen** [M]. The farm at night, a light on in the house; one window light flickers off when you hover Quit.
- **Item pickup popups stack** [S]. "+3 Wood" messages merge instead of spamming.
- **HUD that fades when not needed** [S]. Needs bars only show when dropping or low.

## 16. Accessibility and options

- **Text size option** [S].
- **Reduced horror mode** [S]. Fewer cues for players who want it cozier.
- **Colourblind-friendly highlights** [S]. Distinct shapes, not just colours, for usable tiles.
- **Disable screen effects** [S]. Vignette, flicker and shake toggles.
- **Pause when the window loses focus** [S].
- **Hold vs toggle for running** [S]. If running is added.

## 17. Audio

- **Real recordings** [M]. Replace the generated placeholders.
- **Footstep sounds per surface** [S]. Grass, path, wood (bridge), stone (mine).
- **Music by area and time** [M]. Different loops for the farm, village, woods, mine and night.
- **Positional sounds** [S]. Animals, the river and the well get quieter with distance.
- **A sound that's slightly wrong** [S]. A chicken cluck played backwards, very rarely.

## 18. Art and polish

- **Seasonal palettes** [M]. If seasons happen.
- **Night lighting** [M]. Light from windows, torches and the moon using `PointLight2D`.
- **Animal animations** [S]. Sleeping animation at night, eating animation.
- **Player emotes** [S]. A shiver when a cue happens, a yawn after midnight.
- **Better crop sprites** [M]. Unique sprites for every crop stage.
- **Water animation** [S]. Gentle ripple frames on the water tiles.
- **Footprints in the mud** [S]. The player leaves short-lived prints after rain.

## 19. Secrets and easter eggs

- **The 41st mark** [S]. See section 2.
- **A fish called "you"** [S]. Extremely rare catch; its description is the player's name.
- **Calling your own number** [S]. A hidden option on the phone. It's busy.
- **Konami code on the title screen** [S]. The title changes from "GAME" to something else for a second.
- **Secret room in the mine** [M]. Behind a breakable wall on floor 2, a desk like the one you were fired from.
- **Hidden achievement** [S]. "Never named him."

## 20. Dev and tooling

- **Debug console** [M]. Cheats for testing: give items, set day, spawn animals, force cues.
- **Cue tester** [S]. A key that fires each watcher cue one by one, to check timing.
- **Balance spreadsheet export** [S]. A tool script that dumps every item's buy/sell price to CSV.
- **More tests** [S]. Fishing journal, crop quality, weather, etc., as features land.
- **Save versioning** [S]. Migration steps per save version, so old saves always load.
- **Replace placeholder art gradually** [M]. Keep `generate_assets.gd` as the fallback.
- **Localisation-ready strings** [M]. Move dialogue and item text into translation files.
